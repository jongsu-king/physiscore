Physiscore 3.5.2 corrects the line-shape fit of the auto-fit workspace: the **XPS** tool and the **XRD auto-fit**. Peak tables made with these two in any earlier version (2.0.0 to 3.5.1) should be fitted again. Every other tool, including the XRD analysis window, computes the same numbers as in 3.5.1.

### Fixed
- **The Lorentzian width of each Voigt line was never fitted.** It stayed at half the starting width, so every line was fitted as a Lorentzian of that width and the areas came out too high: on synthetic Pb 4f doublets, by 35.5 % for Gaussian-dominated lines, 20.6 % for mixed and 3.1 % for Lorentzian-dominated lines. The fitted heights sat above the data. Both widths are now fitted. The same doublets give the areas and the Lorentzian width within 0.1 %.
- **Starting widths and limits.** Each line now starts at the width it was detected with (before, 1.64 times wider), and its Gaussian and Lorentzian widths may each grow to three times that width (before, the Gaussian width could grow seven-fold). Without these two changes the freed width sometimes ended in a wrong solution: a pure Lorentzian with a 28 % too large area, or a 4 eV wide component built from noise under the main line.
- **XRD auto-fit next to another reflection.** Each reflection is fitted in a window of ±3.5°. A neighbouring reflection inside that window was not part of the fit and lowered the area (by 18 and 35 % for two reflections 3.5° apart). The window now stops halfway to a neighbour that is at least 10 % as tall; both areas are then right to 0.2 %.
- **Method notes, section 8.** The log-normal σ of the grain sizes is the sample standard deviation of ln d (divisor N − 1), not the maximum-likelihood value; at 400 grains they differ by 0.1 %. The numbers are unchanged.

### Checks
- Nine new rows in the validation table (98 in all) and 19 new tests; every new test fails on 3.5.1.
- On a Pb 4f spectrum with a metallic shoulder (four lines, Poisson noise), the median over 12 noise realisations is 4f7/2 area −1.6 % and Pb⁰/Pb²⁺ 0.092 for 0.095 (3.5.1: +40 % and 0.064). One spectrum on its own scatters more: Pb⁰/Pb²⁺ between 0.074 and 0.144 for 80 % of 40 realisations.

### Known limitations
- **XRD auto-fit smoothing.** The default smoothing (31 points, 0.62° on a 0.02° grid) and baseline widen narrow reflections by 3 to 28 % and lower their areas by 4 to 12 % on a synthetic pattern. Lower the smoothing window before taking Scherrer sizes from the auto-fit table. The XRD analysis window does not use this path.
- The XPS doublet mode still fits each candidate in its own window.
