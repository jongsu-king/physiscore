Physiscore 3.4.0 replaces the Gemini commentary with rule-based interpretation that runs on your computer. Every window's **Interpretation** button now lists findings computed from the results by explicit rules, each with its evidence, the rule or threshold, a literature reference and a suggested next step. The same data always give the same text, nothing is sent over the network, and no account or API key is needed. The numbers are unchanged from 3.3.1; the validation table gives the same values.

### Interpretation without a language model
- One window for every tool: findings sorted worst first (problem, warning, note, good), the evidence, rule, reference and next step of the selected finding, **Copy as text** and **Save report (Markdown)** for the lab notebook or the supplementary information.
- **J-V**: the fill-factor gap split into recombination, series and shunt losses (Green's FF₀, FFs, FFsh), ideality-factor regime, normalised Rs and Rsh, S-shape, hysteresis index. Between conditions: spread (CV), outlier cells (modified z-score), Welch t-test with Holm correction and Hedges' g, and a note when a significant difference is below 1 % of the reference.
- **J-V Dark**: dark ideality, leakage, series resistance, fit quality.
- **EQE**: Urbach energy, current against the detailed-balance limit, where the current is lost (weighted by the AM1.5G photon flux), radiative and non-radiative voltage losses with the implied EQE_EL.
- **Absorbance**: linearity and width of the Tauc region, whether the other transition type fits better, the edge position.
- **PL**: line width in meV and kT, quenching or enhancement against the reference, peak and Stokes shifts.
- **XRD**: Scherrer sizes, Williamson-Hall strain, phase coverage and shift, unassigned reflections checked against common secondary phases and substrate lines (PbI₂, δ-FAPbI₃, FTO, ITO, Au, ...), Harris texture coefficient, crystallinity.
- **FE-SEM**: distribution width, bimodality coefficient, log-normal test, small-grain fraction, process hints.
- **Raman / FTIR**: band assignment from the built-in library, functional groups, shifts and width changes against the reference; *Compare* reads two bands or two band lists.
- **XPS and the auto-fit workspaces**: metallic lead next to Pb²⁺, the Pb 4f doublet ratio and split, I/Pb, the C 1s charge reference, a caution on the O 1s 531-532 eV band; detection and quantification limits (Currie), unresolved neighbours, residual against noise. New **Interpret fit** button; the peak report carries the findings.
- **Device summary**: J-V/EQE current agreement, band-gap agreement, fractions of the detailed-balance limit, the voltage-loss split.

The rules, thresholds and references are listed in the methods document, section 16 (Help → Methods and algorithms).

### Removed
- The Gemini panels, chat and process advisor, and the API-key field in Settings. A key saved by an older version is deleted from the settings file the next time you save the settings. If you created a key only for Physiscore, you can revoke it in Google AI Studio.
- The `google-generativeai` and `pydantic` dependencies; the installers are a little smaller.
