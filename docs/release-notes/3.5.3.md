Physiscore 3.5.3 is a second correction of the auto-fit workspace: the **XPS** tool and the **XRD auto-fit**. Fit XRD and XPS auto-fit tables from 3.5.2 or earlier again if you use their widths (Scherrer sizes, FWHM) or the S/N column. Fit XPS tables made in doublet mode again whatever their version. The PL window's single-band width also changes when the spectrum has an offset (see below). Every other tool, including the XRD analysis window, gives the same numbers as in 3.5.2.

### Fixed
- **Widths came from the smoothed curve.** The smoothing that finds the peaks was also the data the peaks were fitted to. With the XRD default (31 points) a synthetic pattern gave widths 2 to 29 % too large, and 174 % too large for a 0.11° reflection. A 0.2 eV XPS scan gave Pb 4f7/2 7.5 % too wide with 5 % too little area. The lines are now fitted to the unsmoothed data minus the background, and the same widths come out within 3 %. The *Corrected + Fit* view draws these data in grey under the smoothed curve.
- **S/N was too high.** The S/N column and the **Interpret fit** limits used the noise of the smoothed curve. On the synthetic XRD pattern that was 0.93 counts against a real noise of 15, so S/N was 18 times too high and noise peaks passed as measurable lines. They now use the noise of the unsmoothed data: the five real reflections have S/N 35 to 80 and every other row is below 10.
- **XPS doublet mode.** Every detected peak was fitted as the first line of its own doublet, partner lines included. A Pb 4f spectrum with a metallic shoulder came back as four doublets instead of two. A peak one split above a stronger peak is now taken as its partner line, and overlapping doublets are fitted together. Each doublet has a `doublet_id` in the table. Over 40 noise realisations the median 4f7/2 area is 1.2 % low and Pb⁰/Pb²⁺ is 0.100 (true 0.095).
- **Export Excel and Batch Folder in the XPS and XRD auto-fit.** With the pandas version inside the 3.5.2 installers, **Export Excel** wrote an unreadable file and **Batch Folder** stopped before writing `summary.xlsx`. Both work again.
- **False fit warnings in XRD.** On a synthetic pattern with five reflections, **Interpret fit** reported a poor fit in 32 to 37 regions and passed 13 to 16 rows as measurable. Most of those rows were noise peaks whose fit window took in a stronger reflection. Such windows now stop halfway to the stronger reflection: no region is flagged, and only the five reflections pass.
- **Repeated XRD rows.** Weak peaks on the flank of a strong reflection were fitted onto that reflection, so its row could appear three times. Such fits are now dropped.
- **Single-point fits.** Fitted to unsmoothed data, a noise peak could shrink onto one bright point. Every line is now at least two data steps wide (or half its detected width, if that is smaller).
- **PL band width with an offset.** The PL window does not subtract a baseline by default (Raw), and its single-band fit had no constant, so a dark-count offset widened the band: by 7.6 % for an offset of 5 % of the peak. The fit now includes a constant and gives the width within 0.1 % in the same case.
- **Absorbance: Sub-gap offset.** A constant absorbance below the gap (reflection, scattering, substrate) makes the automatic Tauc gap too low: by 10 meV at an offset of 0.02 for a direct edge, by 87 meV for an indirect one. The new **Sub-gap offset** switch (Keep by default, so your numbers do not change unless you choose it) subtracts the absorbance measured far below the gap, which brings both cases within 1 meV. It does not correct interference fringes.
- **Absorbance: band gap above the edge.** When the automatic Tauc region lands above the absorption edge (a scattering background or interference fringes can pull it there), **Interpret** now warns and asks for the expected gap or a hand-set range. Before, it only noted the difference and blamed a tail.

### Checks
- Ten new rows in the validation table (108 in all) and 15 new tests (462 in all). Every new test fails on 3.5.2.

### Known limitations
- **XRD auto-fit areas** come out 5 to 10 % low on the synthetic pattern, because the baseline follows part of the line tails. With the true background subtracted they are within 1 %. Compare areas between patterns processed with the same settings.
- **Automatic Tauc region.** Without the offset switch, the gap stays low as described above (23 meV for a direct edge at an offset of 0.05). A background rising towards short wavelengths can pull the automatic region above the edge; **Interpret** then warns. The region selection itself is unchanged in this version.
