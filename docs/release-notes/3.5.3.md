Physiscore 3.5.3 is a second correction of the auto-fit workspace: the **XPS** tool and the **XRD auto-fit**. Fit XRD and XPS auto-fit tables from 3.5.2 or earlier again if you use their widths (Scherrer sizes, FWHM) or the S/N column. Fit XPS tables made in doublet mode again whatever their version. Every other tool, including the XRD analysis window, gives the same numbers as in 3.5.2.

### Fixed
- **Widths came from the smoothed curve.** The smoothing that finds the peaks was also the data the peaks were fitted to. With the XRD default (31 points) a synthetic pattern gave widths 2 to 29 % too large, and 174 % too large for a 0.11° reflection. A 0.2 eV XPS scan gave Pb 4f7/2 7.5 % too wide with 5 % too little area. The lines are now fitted to the unsmoothed data minus the background, and the same widths come out within 3 %. The *Corrected + Fit* view draws these data in grey under the smoothed curve.
- **S/N was too high.** The S/N column and the **Interpret fit** limits used the noise of the smoothed curve. On the synthetic XRD pattern that was 0.93 counts against a real noise of 15, so S/N was 18 times too high and noise peaks passed as measurable lines. They now use the noise of the unsmoothed data: the five real reflections have S/N 35 to 80 and every other row is below 10.
- **XPS doublet mode.** Every detected peak was fitted as the first line of its own doublet, partner lines included. A Pb 4f spectrum with a metallic shoulder came back as four doublets instead of two. A peak one split above a stronger peak is now taken as its partner line, and overlapping doublets are fitted together. Each doublet has a `doublet_id` in the table. Over 40 noise realisations the median 4f7/2 area is 1.2 % low and Pb⁰/Pb²⁺ is 0.100 (true 0.095).
- **Repeated XRD rows.** Weak peaks on the flank of a strong reflection were fitted onto that reflection, so its row could appear three times. Such fits are now dropped.
- **Single-point fits.** Fitted to unsmoothed data, a noise peak could shrink onto one bright point. Every line is now at least two data steps wide (or half its detected width, if that is smaller).

### Checks
- Six new rows in the validation table (104 in all) and 8 new tests (455 in all). Every new test fails on 3.5.2.

### Known limitations
- **XRD auto-fit areas** come out 5 to 10 % low on the synthetic pattern, because the baseline follows part of the line tails. With the true background subtracted they are within 1 %. Compare areas between patterns processed with the same settings.
