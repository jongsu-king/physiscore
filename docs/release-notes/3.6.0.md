Physiscore 3.6.0 adds instrument-response fitting to **TRPL**, the distribution of relaxation times to **Impedance**, and reads text files saved as "Unicode text" (UTF-16) in every tool. TRPL results from photon-counting files change slightly even without a response, because the fit is now unbiased at low counts (see below). Every other tool gives the same numbers as 3.5.3.

### New
- **TRPL: fit with the instrument response.** Choose *Response* = *gaussian* (the width is fitted, or enter it) or *measured* (pick the response you recorded with a scatterer using **Instrument response file...**). The model convolved with the response is fitted from the rising edge, so components close to the response width are no longer overestimated. On a synthetic decay with 0.3 and 3 ns under a 0.5 ns response, the fit gives 0.307 ns; the tail fit gave 0.409 ns. With a recorded response that has a slow tail, the measured response gives 0.303 ns and its 0.12 ns shift; a Gaussian would give 0.41 ns. The results also list the tail fit of the same trace for comparison.
- **Impedance: distribution of relaxation times (DRT).** A new **DRT** view, rows and Excel sheet show how many relaxation processes the spectrum holds, without choosing a circuit first, and the resistance of each. **Interpretation** compares this with the circuit. On the example spectrum the two processes come out at 200.1 and 1499 Ω (true 200 and 1500).
- **UTF-16 files.** Text exports that some Windows programs save as "Unicode text" failed or read as nonsense in the transport tools, J-V, XRD, Raman, FTIR, PL, EQE and XPS. They are read normally now.

### Changed
- **TRPL fits of counting data use Poisson maximum likelihood.** The previous weighting made both lifetimes about 5 % too short at 1000 peak counts (median of 8 synthetic traces); now within 0.5 %. At 10 000 counts the difference is below 1 %. Fit TRPL files with few counts again if you compare lifetimes between samples.
- **TRPL background.** The automatic background now leaves out the rising edge of the pulse and uses the mean for counts.

### Checks
- 18 new rows in the validation table (126 in all) and 62 new tests (524 in all).

### Known limitations
- Reconvolution fits exponential models only; the stretched exponential stays a tail fit.
- A lifetime below a tenth of the response width (a fifth when the width is fitted) depends on the exact shape of the response; the window then calls it an upper limit.
- The DRT assumes non-negative relaxation processes: it cannot describe an inductive loop (negative capacitance), and a check says so.
