Physiscore 3.2.0 gives every window the look of instrument software. The analysis is
unchanged from 3.1.2; the validation table gives the same values.

### Main window
- A **workbench** replaces the grid of cards: the tools in a tree grouped by category
  with their status on the left; on the right the selected tool with the files it reads,
  the files it writes and the section of the method notes that documents it, the session
  settings (device area, irradiance and temperature defaults, Origin, interpretation,
  settings file, version), the folders recently processed in J-V Curve and J-V Dark
  (double-click to reopen one), and the units and sign conventions.
- Double-click or Enter opens a tool; *Method notes* opens the methods document.

### Every window
- Group boxes have square corners, a hairline border and a grey title strip, like the
  dock panels of instrument software; title rows with small controls (Clear, Overlay)
  become strips too.
- Compact type (one point smaller), flat toolbar buttons in groups separated by rules,
  a hairline under the toolbar and above the status bar.
- Tables: alternating row shading, ruled column headers; the selected row keeps its
  highlight.
- Option menus, check boxes, sliders and text boxes follow the palette instead of
  customtkinter's blue defaults (light and dark).
- Statistic tiles (J-V, J-V dark, FE-SEM) show their values in the text colour; the
  selected J-V parameter is outlined in the accent colour.
- EQE and Tauc plots: titles and read-outs in the plot text colour, annotations in
  square white boxes, placed away from the data.
- The PL, XRD and XPS auto-fit workspaces get the same panels.

### Tests
- Tokens, the customtkinter theme (also after a module resets it) and the
  panel-header pass; 266 tests.
